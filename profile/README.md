### Team Structure

We use **Nested Teams** per academic year to isolate access, keep permission management effortless, and avoid unwanted notifications.

```text
Fontys-BeCreative (Organization)
└── 👥 ay2627 (Parent Team: All 2026-2027 Students)
    ├── 👥 glow
    └── 👥 fasiontech
    └── etc...
```

* **Parent Teams (`ay[YEARS]`):** The entire student intake for that academic year.
* **Child Teams:** Project-specific teams nested inside their parent year.
* **Access Rule:** Each repository is attached only to its child team with **Write** permissions. All other organization members hold **Read-Only** access.

### 📦 Repository Naming & Structure

Repositories follow a `ay[YEARS]-[PROJECT-NAME]` naming format using lowercase kebab-case.

```text

├── 📁 ay2627-lume
```
